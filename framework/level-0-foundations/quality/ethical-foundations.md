- The **UN Fundamental Principles of Official Statistics (FPOS)** provide a global ethical foundation for statistics, emphasising professional standards, impartiality and confidentiality. The CoP operationalises these principles within the ESS.
- The **AI HLEG 2019 Ethical Guidelines for Trustworthy AI** state that AI usage should be lawful, ethical and robust.
- AI-specific guidance for statistics has been published by, among others, Statistics Canada, the UK Statistics Authority, UNECE, Destatis and Statistics Sweden. According to WP 5.1, these documents converge on human oversight, fairness, transparency, robustness, proportionality and accountability.

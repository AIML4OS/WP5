## Introduction to quality

The European Statistical System (ESS) quality policy -- anchored in Regulation (EC) No 223/2009 and operationalized through the European Statistics Code of Practice (CoP) -- is a corner stone of EU statistics ensuring commitment to deliver high quality statistics based on methodological state of the art best practices and in an independent and impartial manner. We examine the interaction between AI/ML-driven innovation and the CoP. We argue that the framework remains fundamentally fit for purpose, as it enables adaptive, risk-sensitive, and context-specific implementation. Crucially, the ESS framework allows flexibility. Quality of statistical products is assessed along five dimensions but not according to rigid thresholds. Instead, the principle of fit for purpose ensures that user needs determine the appropriate balance between criteria. For innovative statistics—especially those based on non-traditional data or AI/ML—the use of proxy indicators and qualitative assurance measures appears as a natural way to guide innovation in waiting for more methodological developments. This adaptability enshrined in the CoP make it resilient to integrating technological change. We argue that AI/ML challenges traditional quality assurance mechanisms, but it also reinforces their importance. Rather than replacing existing frameworks, AI/ML calls for adaptation or extension of the CoP guidance. A key idea therefore consists in specifying the requirements from the CoP for the processing and working methods as well as the products of the statistical offices in such a way that they provide a guide for action and a recital for the use of KI/ML in official statistics. Saidani and Dumpert (2025) already derive quality dimensions for AI/ML in their section "The QAF Indicators and Their Relevance for Machine Learning". This already creates the basis and connection to appropriately expand CoP and QAF.

## The ESS quality architecture

The common ESS quality architecture is a layered framework. The CoP defines the quality principles and indicators. The Quality Assurance Framework (QAF) translates them into assurance practices. Other standards like the The ESS Handbook for Quality and Metadata Reports supports compliance with the European Statistics Code of Practice by providing recommendations and guidance on how to collect evidence and report on quality for the full range of statistical processes and their outputs.

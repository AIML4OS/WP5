- **Regulation (EC) No 223/2009 on European statistics** (Article 2): official statistics must adhere to the principles of professional independence, impartiality, objectivity, reliability, statistical confidentiality and cost-effectiveness. Article 20 requires that data collected for statistical purposes is used only in that context and that all necessary measures are taken to protect confidential data.
- **GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679)** and national data protection acts: personal data may only be processed on a specific and appropriate legal basis, for statistical purposes only.
- **AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)**, in force since August 2024: a risk-based approach that classifies AI systems as unacceptable, high, limited or minimal risk. According to WP 5.1, the production of official statistics is not automatically classified as high-risk, and typical statistical uses (e.g. imputation, classification of survey responses for aggregated outputs) will generally fall into low- or minimal-risk categories. Transparency requirements remain.
- **National statistical acts** apply in addition.

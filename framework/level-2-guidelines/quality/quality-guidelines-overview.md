Quality guidelines serve to make abstract quality dimensions applicable in practice. They thus have a concretising function, clarifying which questions need to be answered in a specific application case when considering a quality dimension. It may well be that not all quality guidelines are applicable to a particular use case or are considered equally important. This introduces the second function: the overview of quality guidelines serves as a checklist. For each application, a status can and should be specified for each guideline: completed/observed, not completed/not observed, not relevant (with justification if necessary). This is an important component in documenting the quality-assured use of AI/ML in the production of official statistics. Note that to facilitate quality assurance throughout the whole application lifecycle the status of guidelines should be periodically reviewed for current application. For example, a completed status needs to be verified again after some period. Finally, a third function is to highlight gaps in research and development. For example, the requirement that ‘all relevant metrics were reported along with suitable uncertainty quantification’ is clear at first glance. However, depending on the situation (e.g. the type of sample used for training), the estimation of suitable confidence intervals, for example, may raise questions that need to be clarified. Further examples are easy to deduce. Within the scope of the AI/ML4OS project, WP 5.2 and WP 5.3 (see Sections below) are already addressing some of these issues. The quality guidelines for the quality dimensions developed above are presented hereinafter.

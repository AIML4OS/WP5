**Short description:** The ability to understand how the results are generated (technically, relationships between variables, meaning of model components).

Explainability is the ability to understand which relationships the algorithm uses to make predictions, i.e. to demonstrate the (possibly local) relationship between input and output variables. Besides post-hoc interpretability, the concept includes *decomposability* (model components have intuitive meanings) and *algorithmic transparency* (the behaviour of the algorithm can be described in technical terms, e.g. convergence or optimality criteria).

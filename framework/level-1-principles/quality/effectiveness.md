**Short description:** The cost effectiveness, added value and proportionality of the model are well understood.

WP 5.1 proposes Effectiveness as a wider dimension than cost effectiveness (Yung et al. 2022; Saidani et al. 2023):

- **Cost effectiveness**: e.g. reduced manual labour in coding and editing, or use of new data sources. Calculations should rely on the established procedures of the NSI.
- **Added value**: improved accuracy or ability to describe accuracy, improved timeliness of the GSBPM step where AI/ML is applied, reduced response burden (CoP Principle 9), improved reproducibility and standardisation.
- **Proportionality**: when several solutions meet the quality requirements, prefer the simpler one (principle of parsimony, "Occam's razor").

The dimension *timeliness and punctuality* from earlier proposals was removed because of its overlap with the corresponding QAF dimension.
